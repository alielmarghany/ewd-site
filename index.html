Selling by DM

Hours of back-and-forth

  • A customer asks the price.
  • You send photos and a price list.
  • They ask about sizes, delivery and payment.
  • You confirm the address by hand.
  • You track the order in your notes.
  • Repeat for every customer.
The result: slow replies, lost orders and you stuck answering the same questions.
With an EWD store

Orders in a few taps

  • Customers browse products, prices and sizes on their own, any time of day.
  • They check out with card, wallet or cash on delivery.
  • You get organized orders with every detail, ready to ship.
The result: more sales, fewer messages, and time back to grow the brand.
